# Thriftly

Thriftly helps shoppers compare physical thrift and resale shops, then get directions. The source of truth for current coverage is `data/stores-us.json`; a listing must represent public retail rather than an office, warehouse, or donation-only stop.

The San Diego independent-store pilot adds a small curated selection of local operators, including nonprofits. Local operators may run more than one regional shop. National brands remain separate filter options. The pilot is not a complete directory of the city.

“Verified online” means a deliberate review of the operator's website for retail shopping, address, and local operation, recorded with source links and a check timestamp. It does not mean a visit, phone confirmation, verified stock, or guaranteed current hours. Imports and refreshes must preserve the original review date.

Search results, street addresses, and directions work independently of the map. Department filters use sourced statements about merchandise categories, never a promise of current inventory. Corrections enter a private moderation queue and cannot directly change public listings.

The Visit score combines available neighborhood Census measures, historical walkability data, and approved Thriftly shopper reviews. It is not an inventory or value prediction. Google ratings are separately attributed when the integration is configured.

## Interface

Extend the established Geist typography, neutral light and dark surfaces, green primary actions, and simple list rows. Prioritize store identity and directions on small screens; keep chain and independent filters distinguishable. Source dates and verification scope remain readable in every locale. The pilot inherits the existing interface rather than creating a new visual identity.
